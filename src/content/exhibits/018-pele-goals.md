## The incident

On 19 December 2020 FC Barcelona tweeted: "Leo #Messi equals @Pele as the all time top goalscorer for one club WITH 643 goals!" On 22 December, after he scored against Valladolid, the club said he had "moved past the legendary Pelé", with "644 goals, all wearing an FC Barcelona shirt, across 17 seasons and 749 games". FIFA had the same 643 for Santos, in 659 matches, in a table dated 1 January 2020.

On 26 December Santos published a statement, signed by Fernando Ribeiro of Assophis, an association of Santos historians: "Pelé marcou 1.091 gols pelo Santos." (Pelé scored 1,091 goals for Santos.) The specialist press, the statement said, counted 643 in competitions, and the 448 goals scored in friendlies were "relegados ao ostracismo" (relegated to ostracism). 643 plus 448 is 1,091.

"Os números são absolutos." (The numbers are absolute.)

## Who lied

Nobody. Each counter counts something different.

Santos's statement says the friendlies were played in official kits, under the official rules, with a match report. "Um gol diante do Transvaal (do Suriname) possui o mesmo valor de um anotado no Real Madrid, em pleno Santiago Bernabéu." (A goal against Transvaal of Suriname has the same value as one scored against Real Madrid, in the Santiago Bernabéu itself.) By the club's count, Santos spent more than 1,000 days touring the planet, and skipped the 1967 Copa Libertadores despite having qualified. The statement also quotes Pelé congratulating Messi: stories like theirs, of love for the same club for so long, "infelizmente serão cada vez mais raras no futebol" (will sadly be ever rarer in football).

RSSSF publishes 4 career counts. In all matches, 1,324 goals in 1,413, 8th on its list. In official matches, which for RSSSF include army and regional selections, 778 in 851, 11th. At what it calls the top level, in 2 lists it marks preliminary, 1,274 in all matches, 1st, and 770 in official ones, 3rd.

CONMEBOL, with data from Opta, gives 1,281 goals in 1,363 matches, which it calls "oficiales". The Guinness record page has the same 1,363 games and 1,279 goals, and does not say what the games include. "He later added two more goals in special appearances."

A FIFA page puts his strikes overall at "1,000-plus".

## The bit continues

Guinness still lists Pelé as the record holder: 1,279 goals, 7 Sep 1956 to 1 Oct 1977. Its obituary, published 29 December 2022, gives the same dates and 1,283 goals. Neither page gives a reason for the difference.

Guinness calls 1959 his best year, with 126, scope unstated. FIFA gives 127 for Santos that year. RSSSF gives 128 in all matches and, in a preliminary list, 120 at the top level. That is 4 totals for 1 year.

Brazil's Lei nº 14.909, of 1 July 2024, makes every 19 November the Dia do Rei Pelé, "data em que marcou seu milésimo gol, no ano de 1969" (the date he scored his 1,000th goal, in 1969).

Federal law dates the 1,000th goal. The counters do not agree on the total.
