## The shape

The same career has 5 published totals from 3 counters: Guinness 1,279 and 1,283, CONMEBOL 1,281, RSSSF 1,324 in all matches and 778 in official ones. FIFA says "1,000-plus". Guinness disagrees with itself: 1,279 on its record page, 1,283 in its own obituary of him, for the same dates. In December 2020 Messi passed Pelé's 643 goals for Santos; 4 days later Santos published 1,091, adding 448 goals from friendlies. Nobody lied: each counter counts something different, and the gap is mostly friendlies.

Recommended category: reuse "the count depended on who was counting" (015). The shape repeats exactly: same events, different counters, different totals.

### Santos FC statement, 26 December 2020
Original: https://www.santosfc.com.br/rei-pele-1-091-gols-pelo-santos-fc/ (returns 403 to scripts; the Internet Archive holds a copy dated 16 June 2026 but rate-limited every attempt). Read in full via Gazeta Esportiva's reproduction, "Santos publica texto para defender Pelé após "recorde" de Messi: "Números são absolutos"", published 26/12/2020 16:45, https://www.gazetaesportiva.com/times/santos/santos-publica-texto-para-defender-pele-apos-recorde-de-messi-numeros-sao-absolutos/ , which introduces it as the text "publicado pelo Santos e assinado por Fernando Ribeiro, da Associação dos Pesquisadores e Historiadores do Santos FC (Assophis)". Attribute quotes to Santos's statement.
Verbatim (Portuguese, then translation):
- "Pelé marcou 1.091 gols pelo Santos." Pelé scored 1,091 goals for Santos.
- "Nas contas da imprensa especializada, o Rei do Futebol marcou 643 em competições e os 448 gols anotados em partidas e competições amistosas foram relegados ao ostracismo, como se tivessem menos valor do que os demais." By the specialist press's count, the King scored 643 in competitions, and the 448 goals scored in friendly matches and competitions were relegated to ostracism, as if they were worth less than the rest.
- "Os números são absolutos." The numbers are absolute.
- "Um gol diante do Transvaal (do Suriname) possui o mesmo valor de um anotado no Real Madrid, em pleno Santiago Bernabéu." A goal against Transvaal (of Suriname) has the same value as one scored against Real Madrid, in the Santiago Bernabéu itself.
- "as partidas foram disputadas com uniformes oficiais, com as regras oficiais do jogo e com súmula." The matches were played in official kits, under the official rules of the game, and with a match report.
- "O alvinegro praiano passou mais de 1000 dias excursionando pelo planeta – que dá um pouco mais de dois anos fora de casa." Santos spent more than 1,000 days touring the planet, a little over 2 years away from home. (Self-reported by Santos; attribute. Don't quote the dash.)
- "Chegou até ao ponto de ignorar a disputa da Copa Libertadores no ano de 1967, mesmo tendo obtido a vaga" It went as far as skipping the 1967 Copa Libertadores, despite having qualified.
- The statement also quotes Pelé's own congratulations to Messi: "Histórias como a nossa, de amor ao mesmo clube por tanto tempo, infelizmente serão cada vez mais raras no futebol" (Stories like ours, of love for the same club for so long, will sadly be ever rarer in football).
- Arithmetic check: 643 + 448 = 1,091.
