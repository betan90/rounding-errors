Research exhibit 018 for Rounding Errors: Pelé's goal count. The shape: the same career, counted by different institutions, gives different totals, and each total is right about what it counts. The main session will read every primary you list itself before briefing the drafter, so give exact URLs, exact figures, and verbatim quotes with where they appear.

What to establish, each against the counter's own publication where one exists:

1. Guinness World Records: the record title, the figure (reported as 1,279 goals in 1,363 games, 7 September 1956 to 1 October 1977), what it includes (friendlies, tours, military or unofficial games?) and what it excludes (reported: 2 goals in "special appearances" for Santos after he retired in 1977). Is Pelé still the holder today, and does Guinness now also list a separate "official goals" record held by someone else?
2. FIFA, and CONMEBOL: the figure each publishes (reported as 1,281 goals in 1,363 games) and where. FIFA's tribute or statements around his death on 29 December 2022 count. Also whether FIFA confirmed 643 goals in 659 matches for Santos in December 2020, and where.
3. Pelé's own count (reported as 1,283 goals in 1,366 matches): his official site, foundation, or official social accounts. Also any statement of his about "official" goals, for example when Cristiano Ronaldo passed him in 2021 (a figure of 757 official goals circulates; find who published it).
4. RSSSF (Rec.Sport.Soccer Statistics Foundation): its Pelé page, its total (reported as 1,284 goals in 1,375 matches) and its official-matches total (reported as 769). Note what RSSSF counts as official.
5. Santos FC: the official Santos-only tally (643 goals in 659 official matches) and the club's statement of about 26 December 2020, after Messi scored his 644th goal for Barcelona, that Pelé scored 1,091 goals for Santos, counting 448 in friendlies, including the line that a goal against Transvaal (Suriname) is worth the same as one against Real Madrid. Find the original on santosfc.com.br or the club's official account, in Portuguese, and translate the quoted lines. News reports are pointers only.
6. FC Barcelona: the dates and opponents of Messi's 643rd and 644th goals for the club (reported as Valencia on 19 December 2020 and Real Valladolid on 22 December 2020), from fcbarcelona.com.
7. The 1,000th goal: date, venue, opponent, and how it was scored (reported as a penalty against Vasco da Gama at the Maracanã on 19 November 1969), from an official source (Santos, CBF, FIFA). Whether the count that reached 1,000 included goals for military or other non-club, non-Brazil teams, if any official source says so.
8. Brazil goals, if it holds: Pelé's 77 goals for Brazil in official matches, a higher total including unofficial matches, and what CBF or FIFA said when Neymar passed 77 in September 2023.
9. Anything a counter has corrected or revised about its own Pelé total, with dates. These go under INSTITUTIONAL ACTS.

For CHARTABLE DATA: list every total with its counter, the scope (career, Santos only, Brazil only), what it includes, and its match count, so a chart can set totals of the same scope side by side without mixing scopes.
